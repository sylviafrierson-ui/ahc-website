What a house call looks like

Dr. Sylvia brings everything with her: a portable treatment table and her adjusting instruments. All she needs is about the length of a person lying down, and the table sets up and comes down in under five minutes. There is nothing to change into; come as you are. Pets are welcome to stay out and say hello.

Your first visit starts with time to talk about your health history, your concerns and your goals, followed by an evaluation. Care uses gentle, precise instruments, with no twisting, popping or cracking. After an upper neck adjustment you simply rest for 20 to 30 minutes in a comfortable position, on your couch or your own bed.
