    Meet your chiropractor

    0:00 / 0:50

    Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC

    Dr. Frierson started At Home Chiropractic on the principle that healthcare should be personal, convenient, and focused on helping people live their lives to the fullest. She is a graduate of Sherman College of Chiropractic.

    Rather than traditional spinal manipulation, she works with gentle, specific, instrument-assisted techniques — the Activator Method® as her primary approach, and Orthospinology for upper cervical cases.

    “My goal is not simply to chase symptoms.”

    Her focus is long-term wellness: supporting spinal function and nervous system balance, particularly for patients dealing with autonomic nervous system dysfunction.

    Away from work she gardens, camps, kayaks, forages, bakes bread and preserves food.

    Technique

    Techniques we use

    Two instrument-assisted techniques. Neither one relies on twisting, popping or cracking.

    Activator Method®

    Her primary technique for full‑spine care. A specialized handheld instrument delivers a quick, low‑force impulse.

    • Used throughout the spine and the extremities
    • No twisting, popping or cracking
    • Comfortable for children, seniors, pregnancy and osteoporosis
    How the Activator works →

    Orthospinology

    Upper cervical care, incorporated when clinically appropriate and supported by imaging. Advanced imaging and precise measurements identify subtle misalignments of the upper neck.

    • Advanced CBCT and digital imaging analysis
    • Gentle, specific upper cervical corrections
    • Incorporates the KH‑2 instrument, tailored to your unique anatomy

    How it works

    Chiropractic care that comes to you

    Three simple steps from booking to your first adjustment. Dr. Frierson brings the table, the instruments and everything else. All you need is about as much floor space as a sofa.

    1. 01

      Book your visit

      Choose a time online. Dr. Frierson comes to your home, your office or wherever fits your day.

    2. 02

      Your consultation and exam

      Your first visit starts with a full health history, a chiropractic exam and a nervous system assessment. Dr. Frierson explains to you what she’s found so you understand what’s going on and why.

    3. 03

      Gentle, precise adjustments

      When your first visit shows it’s appropriate, your adjustment is done with a handheld instrument at a controlled, gentle force, with no twisting or cracking. From there, she builds a care plan around your goals.
