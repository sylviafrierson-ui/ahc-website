Gentle Chiropractic With No Twisting, Popping or Cracking

Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC · At Home Chiropractic

Many people want chiropractic care but do not like the idea of having their neck twisted or cracked. Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC uses gentle, precise instruments instead, at your home or workplace in Greenville, Spartanburg, Gaffney and nearby Upstate towns.

Dr. Sylvia is a Sherman College–trained chiropractor focused on upper cervical care, licensed in South Carolina and North Carolina. She has advanced training in Orthospinology and the Activator Method.

How instrument adjusting feels

With the Activator Method, a small handheld instrument delivers a quick, light pulse to a specific spot. With Orthospinology, the adjustment is precise and planned from careful measurements. Neither involves a twist of the neck.

You simply rest on the table in a comfortable position. No movement is needed from you, and there is no twisting of the neck.

Who chooses gentle care

  • People who have never seen a chiropractor and feel nervous
  • People who did not enjoy traditional adjusting
  • Expecting and new moms, and kids of all ages
  • Anyone who wants care that is calm and unhurried

You are always in control

Dr. Sylvia explains each step before it happens, and you are welcome to ask questions or pause at any time.
