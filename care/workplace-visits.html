Workplace visits

A Chiropractor Who Comes to Your Workplace

Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC · At Home Chiropractic

Long desk days, driving, lifting and standing all take a toll. Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC brings chiropractic care to offices and workplaces across Greenville, Spartanburg, Gaffney and nearby Upstate towns, so you can be seen without leaving work.

Dr. Sylvia is a Sherman College–trained chiropractor focused on upper cervical care, licensed in South Carolina and North Carolina. She has advanced training in Orthospinology and the Activator Method.

How a workplace visit works

All Dr. Sylvia needs is a private space with room for a treatment table, about the length of a person lying down. The table sets up and comes down in under five minutes. She wears business casual and fits in at the office.

There is nothing to change into. You simply rest on the table while she works with gentle instruments, with no twisting, popping or cracking.

Good for

  • Desk and computer workers
  • Business owners who cannot step away for long
  • Teams who want several people seen in one visit
  • Remote workers who want a visit at their home office
