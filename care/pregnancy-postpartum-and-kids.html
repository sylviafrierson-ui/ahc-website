For the whole family

Gentle Chiropractic for Expecting Moms, New Moms and Kids

Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC · At Home Chiropractic

Pregnancy, a new baby and growing kids all change how bodies move. Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC offers gentle chiropractic care for expecting moms, new moms and kids of all ages, at home in Greenville, Spartanburg, Gaffney and nearby Upstate towns.

Dr. Sylvia is a Sherman College–trained chiropractor focused on upper cervical care, licensed in South Carolina and North Carolina. She has advanced training in Orthospinology and the Activator Method.

During pregnancy

As the body changes, care focuses on comfort, posture, and alignment and balance of the pelvis, and it adapts as your body changes from month to month. Positioning is adjusted for your comfort, and the instruments Dr. Sylvia uses mean there is no twisting or popping.

Always keep your OB or midwife in the loop about any care you receive.

After the baby arrives

Feeding, rocking, lifting a car seat and bending over a crib add up. A house call means you do not have to pack up the baby or find a sitter. You can be seen in your own living room, and the baby can stay right with you.

Kids of all ages

Children are seen with the same gentle, instrument-based approach, adapted to their size, in a familiar setting. Parents can stay right there, and Dr. Sylvia explains each step before it happens. Several family members can be seen in the same visit.
