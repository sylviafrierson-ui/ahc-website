Upper cervical chiropractic focuses on the top of the neck, where the skull meets the spine. Dr. Sylvia Frierson, DC brings this gentle, precise kind of care to homes and workplaces across Greenville, Spartanburg, Gaffney and nearby Upstate towns.

Dr. Sylvia is a Sherman College–trained chiropractor focused on upper cervical care, licensed in South Carolina and North Carolina. She has advanced training in Orthospinology and the Activator Method.

What makes upper cervical care different

The top bone of the neck, the atlas, holds up the head, and it surrounds the brainstem, where the brain connects to the spinal cord. Upper cervical chiropractors pay close attention to how this area is aligned and how the head is balanced on the neck.

Rather than adjusting many parts of the spine at each visit, upper cervical care is careful and specific. Dr. Sylvia uses measurements and a thorough evaluation to decide what, if anything, to adjust.

Orthospinology and the Activator Method

Dr. Sylvia uses two instrument-based techniques. Orthospinology is a precise upper cervical approach, and the Activator Method uses a small handheld instrument to deliver a quick, gentle adjustment. Neither involves twisting, popping or cracking.

Why at home?

After an upper neck adjustment, people rest for 20 to 30 minutes in a comfortable position. At home, that rest happens on your own couch or bed instead of in the car on the way back from a clinic.

Dr. Sylvia brings a portable table and her instruments. All she needs is about the length of a person lying down.
